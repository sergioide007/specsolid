Solid software, specified before it is written.

SpecSolid brings together the wisdom of the people who founded modern software engineering and the power of AI: you write what you want before you write how, and every module respects the SOLID principles.

Principles

Five ideas hold everything else up.

  1. 01

    Spec-first

    You write what you want before you write how. A simple spec.md with checkboxes you can verify.

  2. 02

    SOLID-aligned

    Every module respects the principles of good design. With AI they matter more, not less.

  3. 03

    Community-driven

    Knowledge belongs to everyone. Everything is free and everything is open.

  4. 04

    AI-augmented

    Humans write the specs; AI helps execute them.

  5. 05

    Privacy-respecting

    By design, not as a compliance checkbox.

FAQ

The essentials, briefly.

What is SpecSolid?

A philosophy, a framework and a community for building software with AI. It starts from a simple idea: before you write code, you write a specification, and every module respects the SOLID principles. The name is a tribute to Robert C. Martin's SOLID principles.

What is Spec-Driven Development (SDD)?

It is SpecSolid's core practice: write a short, verifiable specification first (a spec.md file with checkboxes) and use it to guide people, the AI and the tests. When something fails, you fix the spec and regenerate, instead of patching the code by hand.

Is AI replacing developers?

No. It amplifies them. A developer with good principles and good tools gets far more done than one without them, but model-generated code still needs people who design, review and maintain it.

On Amazon in Kindle, paperback and hardcover editions. You can read a free sample at info-codigo-sintetico.specsolid.com/en/. The original Spanish edition, Código Sintético, is also on Lulu, Apple Books, Kobo and Gumroad.

Nothing. The tools, the DevOps curriculum, the theory framework and the extended book series are free and open source. Only the published book is sold.
