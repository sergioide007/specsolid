A philosophy, a framework and a community, built on the shoulders of giants.

Before we talk about what SpecSolid is, we need to talk about the people who made it possible. Any honest description of a software framework must acknowledge its intellectual heritage.

The masters who shaped modern software

Robert C. Martin: the battle against bad code

Clean Code (Martin, 2008) taught a generation of developers that code is not just for computers: it is for humans, who read it far more often than they write it.

His SOLID principles (Single Responsibility, Open/Closed, Liskov Substitution, Interface Segregation and Dependency Inversion) are the architectural DNA of SpecSolid. Every decision in this framework asks: does this respect SOLID? The very name SpecSolid is a tribute to his work.

Martin Fowler: the architect of patterns

Patterns of Enterprise Application Architecture (Fowler, 2002) and Refactoring (Fowler, 2018) established that good software has patterns that can be learned, taught and shared. His idea of evolutionary architecture, systems designed to change rather than to be perfect on day one, is central to SpecSolid: we do not try to predict the future; we write specs that let us adapt to it.

Kent Beck: TDD and Extreme Programming

Test-driven development (Beck, 2002) teaches us to write the test before the code. That small inversion of habit forces us to think about what success looks like before we build. It is, in essence, a form of specification. In SpecSolid, specifications are written before code: the same philosophy, broader scope.

The 17 authors of the Agile Manifesto

In February 2001, seventeen software thinkers met at Snowbird, Utah, and wrote the Manifesto for Agile Software Development (Beck et al., 2001): four values and twelve principles that moved the industry from heavy, bureaucratic processes toward iterative, human-centered delivery. Among them were Ward Cunningham, inventor of the wiki; Ron Jeffries, co-creator of Extreme Programming; Alistair Cockburn, creator of the Crystal methods; Andy Hunt and Dave Thomas, authors of The Pragmatic Programmer; and Ken Schwaber and Jeff Sutherland, co-creators of Scrum.

"Working software over comprehensive documentation" does not mean no documentation: it means documentation that serves the work. That is what a good spec is.

The architects of the AI era

If the previous generation gave us principles for building software, the current one is teaching us how to build intelligence.

  • Geoffrey Hinton, Yoshua Bengio and Yann LeCun, the godfathers of deep learning, pursued for decades a line of research most of academia dismissed. In 2024 Hinton received the Nobel Prize in Physics, alongside John Hopfield.
  • Ilya Sutskever, with Hinton and Krizhevsky, showed with AlexNet (2012) that deep neural networks could outperform every previous approach to image recognition.
  • Ashish Vaswani and his co-authors introduced the Transformer (2017), the architecture behind today's language models.
  • Sam Altman and the OpenAI team showed with GPT-4 (2023) that language models are general-purpose reasoning engines.
  • Dario and Daniela Amodei and the Anthropic team introduced Constitutional AI (Bai et al., 2022): aligning models with human values through explicit principles. Claude is one of the providers built into Alpaquitay AI.
  • Andrej Karpathy has taught hundreds of thousands of engineers how neural networks work from first principles.
  • Andrew Ng, Harrison Chase and Jerry Liu are defining the agentic era: reflection, tool use, planning and multi-agent collaboration patterns; LangChain; and LlamaIndex for connecting models to your own data. Alpaquitay AI's skill system is a direct implementation of these ideas.

The SpecSolid philosophy

SpecSolid is a philosophy and framework for building software that is:

  • Spec-first. You write what you want before you write how. A simple spec.md with checkboxes you can verify.
  • SOLID-aligned. Every module respects the principles of good design. With AI they matter more, not less.
  • Community-driven. Knowledge belongs to everyone. Everything is free and everything is open.
  • AI-augmented. Humans write the specs; AI helps execute them.
  • Privacy-respecting. By design, not as a compliance checkbox.

Specify before you code

The central idea is simple: before you write a single line of code, write a specification. Not a hundred-page requirements document: a simple spec.md file with checkboxes.

# Feature: User Authentication

- [x] User can register with email and password
- [x] User receives a confirmation email
- [x] User can log in with correct credentials
- [ ] Login fails with incorrect password (401)
- [ ] User can reset password via email link

Alpaquitay AI tracks that spec: the AI reads it, Git commits are correlated to its items and the board visualizes its progress. This is working software emerging from clear intent, exactly what the Agile Manifesto was pointing toward.

No accidental complexity

The ecosystem's sites share a single search and assistant module written in plain JavaScript with no dependencies. No React, no Vue, no framework overhead. The web is the most democratized platform in history, and standard JavaScript runs everywhere, for everyone. This is Fred Brooks's distinction between essential and accidental complexity (Brooks, 1986) applied in practice: we eliminated the accidental kind.

Why this matters

AI is not replacing developers. It is amplifying them. A developer with good principles and good tools is becoming far more productive than one without them, and the gap grows every month. SpecSolid exists to close it, combining the timeless wisdom of Martin, Fowler and Beck, the power of large language models, the emerging patterns of agentic AI and a commitment to free, open, accessible education.

An invitation

  • If you are a student just beginning: you are not too late. The field is changing, and that means there are no established experts yet in what is coming. Start now.
  • If you are a teacher: these tools can augment your courses. The interview assistant can be embedded in your learning platform and the search can be forked and customized. Everything is open.
  • If you are a professional engineer: challenge your assumptions. The SOLID principles matter more, not less, in the age of AI.
  • If you are an expert: share what you know. Martin, Fowler, Beck and Cunningham did not just write code. They wrote books, gave talks and built communities.
